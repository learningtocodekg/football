Decide your move for THIS 0.1s step. You are stateless — you were just dropped into this moment, so
read the field and reason it through before you move. Work these in order:

1. WHAT HAVE I DONE SO FAR? Read the MOVE LOG (your moves and the CB's reactions). How deep am I,
   what is my speed, and has the CB's Δhdg shown I've actually turned his hips — or is he still sitting
   on me?

2. WHAT DOES THE ROUTE CALL FOR NOW? Re-read the ROUTE shape, its PURPOSE, and BREAK DEPTH. Am I still
   stemming or am I at my break? When is my next break? What is this route trying to do to the CB?

3. HAVE I EARNED THE BREAK? Do I have the speed and space advantage the purpose wants? If NOT, I still
   have setup to do — sell the fake the route calls for (ONE decisive move, not a weave back and forth)
   to move him, or keep building speed. If I have ALREADY created that advantage earlier, don't re-stem
   — make my one decisive break and call for the ball on that same step so the throw leads me in stride.

Don't call on the pre-snap cushion — that gap isn't earned. Call as/just-before your FINAL break, when
you expect to be open the next moment.

Output JSON:
{
  "heading": <deg you move this step>,
  "facing": <deg you face; usually = heading>,
  "throttle": "accelerate" | "coast" | "brake",
  "call_for_ball": false,
  "reasoning": "<one short line>"
}

When you decide to call for the ball, set call_for_ball=true and ADD an end_route:
{
  "heading": <final break direction>,
  "facing": <deg>,
  "throttle": "accelerate",
  "call_for_ball": true,
  "end_route": {
    "heading": <exact direction you commit to>,
    "mode": "run" | "settle",
    "settle_ticks": <int, only if mode is settle>
  },
  "reasoning": "<why now>"
}
