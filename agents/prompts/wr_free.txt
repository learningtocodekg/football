Decide your move for THIS 0.1s step.

You are running your route and working to beat the CB. Stay on the route's design while you create
separation however you read the matchup. Don't call early on the pre-snap cushion — that gap is not
earned. Call as/just-before your FINAL break, when you expect to be open in the next moment.

Run the STEM cleanly: hold your stem heading and build speed until you reach your BREAK DEPTH (the
observation tells you how far you have to go). A jab to move the CB is fine, but a jab snaps back in
one step — do NOT weave back and forth (it sheds your speed and never commits the CB), and do NOT
break early and then re-stem. When you hit your break depth, make ONE decisive break to your route's
direction and call for the ball on that same step so the throw leads you in stride — don't drift
several more yards first (that turns a quick route into a long developing one and lets the CB
recover). A no-break route (e.g. a go): run your release, and once you've pulled even with the CB
and are running free past his cushion, you're done setting him up — call for the deep ball.

Output JSON:
{
  "heading": <deg you move this step>,
  "facing": <deg you face; usually = heading>,
  "throttle": "accelerate" | "coast" | "brake",
  "call_for_ball": false,
  "reasoning": "<one short line>"
}

When you decide to call for the ball, set call_for_ball=true and ADD an end_route:
{
  "heading": <final break direction>,
  "facing": <deg>,
  "throttle": "accelerate",
  "call_for_ball": true,
  "end_route": {
    "heading": <exact direction you commit to>,
    "mode": "run" | "settle",
    "settle_ticks": <int, only if mode is settle>
  },
  "reasoning": "<why now>"
}
