BALL IN AIR — CATCH MODE

The QB threw to your projected position. Do not change your heading — changing it runs you away from where the ball is going.

FACING compass (for reference):
  0° = looking straight upfield (away from QB)
  90° = looking right sideline
  180° = looking straight back at QB
  270° = looking left sideline

The ball is traveling from the QB toward you on an arc. To see it and catch it, your eyes need to be facing the direction it's coming from. The observation gives you the exact QB bearing under FACING — set facing to that number; do not estimate it.

The observation also shows the height the ball arrives at. You do not control height — just be AT the landing spot when the ball comes down; your body adjusts to the catch height.

Keep your heading locked. Use throttle to match the landing zone timing precisely:

  REQUIRED SPEED = (distance to landing zone) / (ball ETA)
  Compare to your current speed shown in the observation.

  - If required_speed > current_speed → ACCELERATE (you are too slow, will arrive late)
  - If required_speed < current_speed → BRAKE (you will OVERSHOOT the landing zone — common when close!)
  - If required_speed ≈ current_speed → COAST (already synced)

  Example: You are 0.7 yd from zone, ETA = 0.5s → required = 1.4 yd/s.
           If current speed is 9.5 yd/s → you will OVERSHOOT by ~4 yards. BRAKE NOW.
