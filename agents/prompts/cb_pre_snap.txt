PRE-SNAP ALIGNMENT

Before the play starts, choose where to line up relative to the WR.

OPTIONS:
- offset_yards: how many yards off the line of scrimmage to align behind the WR (0 = press, 5 = off, 8+ = deep)
- side: "inside" (between WR and field center), "outside" (outside of WR), or "press" (directly over WR)

Consider:
- WR's starting position and field position
- Whether you want to take away inside or outside routes
- How fast you are vs. the WR (press is risky against speed)

Respond with JSON only:
{"offset_yards": 5, "side": "outside", "reasoning": "..."}
