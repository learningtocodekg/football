PRE-SNAP ALIGNMENT

Before the play starts, choose where to line up relative to the WR.

ALIGNMENT OPTIONS — pick offset_yards based on the coverage you want:

PRESS (offset_yards 0–1):
  You are in the WR's face at the line of scrimmage.
  PROS: physically disrupt the release; WR cannot get a clean stem; short routes (drag, zig, slant) are contested from the start.
  CONS: a fast WR who gets a clean release burns you deep; if WR makes a sharp inside or outside release move off the line, you over-commit and they are gone; your recovery after a beaten press is large.
  USE WHEN: WR is not significantly faster than you; route expectation is short or quick; you want to dictate timing.

OFF (offset_yards 2–3):
  Balanced coverage — close enough to react, far enough to see the release.
  PROS: can contest both short breaks and medium routes; keeps burst available; WR gets no free release but you still have time to read.
  CONS: gives up a clean short throw on quick routes (drag, slant); WR has a half-step head start.
  USE WHEN: down/distance is balanced; you want to play both short and intermediate.

CUSHION (offset_yards 4–6):
  Soft deep coverage — you are reading and reacting.
  PROS: very hard to beat deep; time to read the stem and burst direction; forces QB to throw short.
  CONS: short routes and quick slants are free yards; WR gets full speed buildup off the line.
  USE WHEN: 3rd & long; protecting against a deep shot; WR is significantly faster than you.

side:
  "inside" — shade between WR and field center (take away inside breaking routes: slant, post, dig)
  "outside" — shade outside the WR (take away outside breaking routes: out, corner, fade)
  "press" — directly over the WR (use with offset_yards 0–1 only)
