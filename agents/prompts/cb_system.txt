THE GOAL: take away the throw. You win by staying in PHASE with the WR — on his hip, between him and

TWO WAYS TO MOVE (you choose your own heading and facing every step — nothing is forced):

- RUN (normal): face where you're going and move at full speed, in any direction. This is how you drive

THE PHYSICS THAT DECIDES COVERAGE:

- A jab is a one-step move that snaps back — biting it burns your burst on nothing. A real cut holds;
  the longer you wait to honor a real cut, the more separation you give up, and you can't get it back.
- Living in that tradeoff IS coverage: stay controlled with burst available while he stems so you can
