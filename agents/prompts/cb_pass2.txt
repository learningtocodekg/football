BALL IN AIR — CHOOSE YOUR INTENT

The ball has been thrown. You have a limited window before it lands. Your observation shows the
landing zone, ball ETA, and your distance to the zone.

STEP 1 — CAN YOU RACE TO THE LANDING ZONE?

Your observation shows:
  - Distance to landing zone (yards)
  - Your top sprint speed (yd/s)
  - Ball ETA (seconds)

Compute: sprint_time = distance_to_zone / your_max_speed

If sprint_time < ball_ETA: YOU CAN GET THERE. But first, ask one more question:

  WHERE IS THE WR RELATIVE TO THE LANDING ZONE?
  Compute: wr_dist_to_zone = distance from WR's current position to the landing zone.
           wr_eta_to_zone = wr_dist_to_zone / WR's current speed (estimate from observation).

  If wr_eta_to_zone < sprint_time: WR arrives first and is already SET at the catch point.
    You are racing the WR but losing. Arriving at the same coordinates as an established WR means
    you are out-of-position — the WR out-leverages you for the ball. Consider play_man instead.
    Exception: if you arrive BEFORE the WR (sprint_time < wr_eta_to_zone), you own the zone.

  If sprint_time << wr_eta_to_zone (you arrive well before the WR): TAKE THE BALL.
    Sprint to the zone, face the ball, contest or intercept.
    Choose swat or go_for_pick — you have zone control.

If sprint_time >= ball_ETA: you cannot reach the zone in time. Go to STEP 2.

STEP 2 — CURRENT ARM REACH (if already close to ball path):

  - Your arm tip (swat, 1.0 yd reach) distance to zone shown in observation.
  - Your arm tip (INT, 0.5 yd reach) distance to zone shown in observation.
  - You must be facing within 60° of the landing zone for swat/INT.

  - Arm tip ≤ 1 yd from zone AND facing OK: swat viable.
  - Arm tip ≤ 0.5 yd from zone AND facing OK: go_for_pick viable.

STEP 3 — PLAY MAN (fallback):

  Use this when you cannot reach the zone AND your arm is not in range.
  - No facing requirement.
  - You must be within 1.0 yd of the WR at catch for any effect.
  - If you are 2+ yd from the WR when the ball arrives: WR catches clean. play_man does nothing.
  - Closer body hit = more likely drop.

OPTIONS SUMMARY:
  "go_for_pick" — sprint to zone or INT from current position (0.5 yd reach, must face ball). High reward.
  "swat"        — sprint to zone or PBU from current position (1.0 yd reach, must face ball). Reliable disruption.
  "play_man"    — shadow WR's body, no ball attempt. Only works within 1.0 yd of WR at catch.
