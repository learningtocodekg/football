CLEARING THE CB - ARC vs LANE: look at where the CB is right now relative to the straight line from you to the catch point.
- If the CB is BETWEEN you and your WR (in the throwing lane - trailing the WR on a deep ball, or sitting underneath the route), a flat bullet flies right through his reach and he tips or picks it. Throw a LOB (loft, or touch) that arcs OVER his head and drops down to your WR beyond him. This is the standard deep-ball-over-the-top throw: WR past the CB, CB now between you and the WR -> loft it over.
- Only use a flat bullet when the lane to your WR is CLEAR of the CB (he is off to the side, or your WR has won outside).

SETTLE / HOOK ROUTES (comeback, curl): the window is BRIEF - the WR is stopping and the CB drives back down onto him fast. Throw a firm bullet to the settle spot the instant he plants/hooks; do not wait for "more" separation, it only shrinks from here.

Your landing spot (target_coord) should be that arc's meeting point. You may shade it a little - a touch further along his path to lead him, or to the side away from the CB so his body shields it - but keep it within ~1 yd of the meeting point or he cannot run onto it.

Catch height (target_z, default 1.5 = chest): raise it toward 2.5-3.0 only to drop the ball where a tight CB cannot reach.

If no arc arrives while he is open, hold and read again next step.
