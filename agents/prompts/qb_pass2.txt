HOW TO PICK THE ARC:
- Flatter (bullet/drive) = arrives sooner = less time for the CB to close. ALWAYS prefer the
  flattest arc that (a) is in range and (b) clears the CB in the lane.
- If the lane note says TIP/PICK RISK, that arc goes through the CB's reach mid-flight — pick a
  higher arc or a different target side.
- Higher (touch/loft) = hangs longer. Only worth it to clear an underneath defender or for max
  range. While the ball hangs, the CB closes 3-4 yd per extra half-second.

CATCH HEIGHT (target_z, optional — default 1.5):

THROW TARGET PLACEMENT — READ THIS:
The landing spot (target_coord) does not need to be exactly at the WR's center. The WR can catch
any ball within 1.3 yards of their projected position. Use that catch circle deliberately:

1. LEAD by 0.3–0.5 yd: shift the target 0.3–0.5 yards AHEAD of the WR's projected position in
   their movement direction. The WR is running at full speed — throw where they WILL BE one step
   later, not where the table shows them now. The WR should run INTO the ball, not stop and wait.
   Use 0.3 yd lead on short/quick throws; 0.5 yd lead on deep routes or immediately post-cut
   (when WR is still accelerating out of recovery and will gain ground before the ball arrives).

2. AWAY from the CB by 0.3–0.5 yd: place the target on the side OPPOSITE the CB. The WR's body
   shields the ball. Be specific about direction:
   - CB left of WR's path → add 0.3–0.5 yd to the RIGHT of WR's projected x.
   - CB right of WR's path → add 0.3–0.5 yd to the LEFT.
   - CB underneath (between WR and QB, closing from behind) → add 0.3–0.5 yd extra lead upfield
     AND raise target_z by 0.3–0.5 yd so the ball arrives over the CB's outstretched arms while
     the WR high-points it.
   - Combined: a 0.4 yd lead + 0.3 yd lateral = 0.5 yd total shift — comfortably inside the
     1.3 yd catch radius and out of the CB's frame.

3. Stay within catch radius: your combined lead + away offset must keep target_coord within ~1.0 yd
   of the WR's projected arrival position. Do not over-adjust — a shift exceeding 1.0 yd risks an
   incompletion regardless of coverage. The goal is the outskirts of the WR's frame, not a wild
   overthrow.

WHAT "OPEN" MEANS AT ARRIVAL:
The CB needs to be within arm reach (~1.0 yd center-to-center) to contest the catch.
- Sep ≥ 1.8 yd at arrival → CB arm cannot reach — clean window, throw it
- Sep 1.2–1.8 yd at arrival → contested but catchable with good placement away from the CB (or high z)
- Sep < 1.0 yd at arrival → CB is body-on-body — very low probability unless you high-point it
