STEP 1 of 2 - IS THERE A WINDOW?

Your WR is locked on his break. Project both players forward from the positions, headings, and speeds above, and from the trend in the recent-movement table: the WR keeps running his locked heading; the CB chases from where he is.

Is a window coming - a stretch of time in the next ~0.3 to 1.5 seconds when the WR will have 1.8+ yd of separation - that you could land the ball in? The ball needs travel time, so the real question is "will he be open soon, when, and where."

- If there is NO clear window yet: hold. You get a fresh read 0.1s later.
- If there IS: give the time span he will be open and the spot to aim (where he will be during that span).
