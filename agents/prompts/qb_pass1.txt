STEP 1 of 2 - IS THE WR OPEN, AND WHEN?

Your WR is locked on his break. From the positions, headings, and speeds above and the trend in the recent-movement table, project both players forward YOURSELF: the WR keeps running his locked heading at roughly his current speed; the CB chases from where he is now.

Decide whether a window is coming - a stretch of time when the WR will have 1.8+ yd of separation from the CB. Account for ball travel time: you are looking ~0.3 to 2.5 seconds ahead.

Watch the DIRECTION separation is moving, not just its size. If the CB is downfield of your WR (in front, in his cushion) and the separation is SHRINKING in the trend table, that gap is the cushion closing - it is NOT a throwing window, because a ball thrown past the CB drops where he is already waiting. On a deep route a real window opens only after the WR pulls EVEN WITH/PAST the CB and separation starts GROWING again; report THAT later span, not the closing cushion. (This applies ONLY to a ball thrown PAST the CB. If your WR is breaking BACK to settle SHORT of the CB - a comeback or curl - the opposite is true: a downfield CB is good, underneath is open, so report the window NOW as he plants - do not wait for him to pass anyone.)

- If you do NOT see a clean window: hold. You get a fresh read 0.1s later.
- If you DO: report WHEN he is open as a time span in SECONDS FROM NOW. You do not pick a spot - once you know when he is open, step 2 shows you where each throw type lands on his path.
