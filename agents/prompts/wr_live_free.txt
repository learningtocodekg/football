ROUTE RUNNING DECISION

=== STRATEGIC FRAMEWORK — think through this every step ===

1. WHERE ARE YOU? Compare your current position to your snap position. How far into the route?
   Where does the route description say you should be right now?

2. WHERE IS THE CB? Upfield, behind, left, right of you? What heading is the CB running?
   Is the CB's body already committed one direction?

3. WHAT DO YOU NEED TO DO NEXT? Check YOUR HEADING NOW in the observation.
   Are you in the stem phase or already in the final break?

4. HOW MUCH DECEPTION DO YOU NEED?
   - Short routes (~5 yards): the stem IS the deception. One window is enough.
   - Longer routes (~10-12 yards): maybe one additional fake. No more.
   - Once the CB's hips are committed the wrong way, STOP faking and execute the route.
   - Constant deception wastes steps and costs you burst when you need it most.

5. UNDERSTAND WHAT "SEPARATION" ACTUALLY MEANS:
   The body gap you see RIGHT NOW does not tell you whether you are open.
   A 5-yard body gap where the CB has FULL BURST is NOT separation — the CB can close it in one second.
   The pre-snap cushion (CB lining up 5 yards off) is NOT earned separation. You have done nothing yet.

   TRUE separation = a gap the CB physically cannot close because their hips are committed the wrong way.
   This only exists AFTER your stem has pulled the CB's weight upfield AND you have made your final cut.
   - CB rec > 0 after your cut = their hips MAY be committed wrong. That is a CANDIDATE window — but
     check what caused the recovery (see MOVE LOG below).
   - CB rec = 0 and full burst = they can close freely regardless of gap size. You are not open yet.

   Body gap >= 1.5 yards WITH CB rec > 0 = high-probability catch. Body gap alone means nothing.

6. WHEN TO CALL FOR BALL:
   Call 0.1-0.3s before you expect TRUE separation — which means one of exactly two windows:
   - Window A: You are 0.1-0.2s BEFORE your final cut. You are confident the cut will commit their hips.
     Call just before the cut so the QB is throwing as you break open.
   - Window B: You JUST MADE your final cut and CB rec > 0. The gap is peaking right now.
     Call immediately — it closes once they recover.

   Do NOT call during the stem. Here is why: during the stem the CB is reading you and tracking upfield.
   If you cut early (before the stem commits their hips), they have FULL BURST and can mirror your break.
   The route only creates separation if the stem ran long enough to make them lean the wrong way first.
   Calling during the stem means the ball arrives while you are still in the middle of a contested route.

   VERTICAL/GO ROUTES (no cut planned — route says "run straight the entire play"):
   There is no stem and no cut — separation comes from pure speed alone. The CB closes toward you
   the entire flight (~1.5–2s). Call only when the CB cannot physically close the gap during flight:
   - CB must be BEHIND you (heading 0°, you are upfield) and at least 5-6 yd back.
   - CB must have ZERO burst recovery advantage — if CB has full burst, they are 1s from closing 9yd.
   - A 3-4 yd gap with CB in full burst is NOT enough — on a 45yd deep ball they close it easily.
   - Call when you have clearly outrun the CB and the gap is growing, not shrinking.

=== HOW DECEPTION WORKS ===

BURST STATUS — read this first:
  CB BURST "RECOVERING — X steps left" -> CB cannot accelerate freely. This is your window.
  CB BURST "FULL burst available" -> CB is set and can match any move instantly. Earn a window first.
  YOUR BURST "RECOVERING" -> you just cut and are paying the cost. Do not snap again yet.

MOVE LOG — read the rec column:
  CB rec > 0 last step: your previous move MAY have cost the CB their burst — but ask WHY.
    GENUINE COMMIT: CB heading in the 2-3 steps BEFORE rec appeared was significantly different from
      your break direction (e.g., CB was heading upfield at ~0° and you are about to break sideways).
      That means the CB was running the wrong way and had to turn hard. THAT is your window.
    TRACKING WOBBLE: CB heading in those steps was approximately the same as yours (CB following you,
      headings within ~45° of each other). Their rec came from a minor step-adjustment while staying
      with you — they are still positioned to contest. This is NOT a committed hip. Wait.
    Rule: Check the last 2-3 CB headings before rec appeared. If the CB was running roughly the same
      direction as you, their recovery is tracking noise, not commitment.
  CB rec = 0 every step: your fakes are not landing. Fake wasn't deep enough, or CB isn't reacting.
  YOUR rec > 0: you just cut. Let it clear before another cut.

FAKE DEPTH:
  A 10-30 degree drift is noise — the CB ignores it. A real fake must reach ~270deg (left) or
  ~90deg (right). Check the move log: if CB rec stayed 0 after your "fake," it wasn't deep enough.

FAKE PURPOSE:
  Every fake has ONE goal: commit the CB's hips the WRONG way for your FINAL break direction.
  If your final break is 45deg, fake toward ~225deg (opposite) first.
  Faking aimlessly wastes steps and leaves the CB free to react.

One line, under ~120 chars. Required content: deception status + current action + next plan.

Format: "[deception: done/needed/not needed yet] | [what you are doing NOW] | [what you plan NEXT]"

Examples:

  "deception: done at t=0.9 (CB rec=3 after jab) | on final break 45deg | calling now, gap peaks in ~0.2s"
  "deception: needed — CB rec=0 after fake, fake didn't land | going deeper to 270deg | snap break next step if CB commits"
  "deception: done | called at t=1.1 | accelerating to catch point on 45deg heading"

NOTE GROUNDING: your note must be consistent with the move log. If the log has no rows yet,
you have taken zero actions — start fresh. If the log shows your heading never left 0deg but
your note says "jabbed left," the note is wrong — correct it.

=== CALL RULES ===

STEP 0 — ROUTE PHASE CHECK (read this first every step):
  Look at "YOUR HEADING NOW" in the observation.
  - If it says "~Xs remaining, then cut to Yd" → you are in an INTERMEDIATE phase. Do NOT call.
    The upcoming cut is setup for the real route, not the route itself. Calling here means the ball
    arrives before the actual break happens — the CB will be in perfect position to contest.
    No matter what the CB is doing, no matter the body gap: you cannot call yet.
  - If it says "this is your final break. Run it." → you are on the actual route. Now evaluate calling.

Call 0.1-0.3s before you expect TRUE separation. Valid windows:
  - 0.1-0.2s before your final cut: you have run the stem, CB hips are being committed upfield, cut is imminent.
  - Just after your final cut: CB rec > 0 confirms hips are committed wrong — AND the MOVE LOG check
    (above) confirms it was a genuine commit, not a tracking wobble. Gap is peaking. Call now.

CRITICAL — HEADING WHEN CALLING:
  Your "heading" output on the call step is LOCKED for the rest of the play. The QB throws to your
  projected trajectory based on that heading. Output the WRONG heading and the ball goes to the
  wrong spot — no catch is possible.

  The TIMING rules above (0.1-0.2s before cut, or just after cut) do NOT change. Only the heading.

  Window A (calling 0.1-0.2s BEFORE your final cut):
    You are still running the stem heading. But your heading output must be your BREAK HEADING —
    the direction you are about to cut to — NOT your current stem heading.
    Example: stem is 0°, final cut to 45° (slant). Call step output: heading=45.
    Example: stem is 0°, final cut to 180° (curl). Call step output: heading=180.
    The QB sees your break heading and throws to where you will be running after the cut.
    Outputting your stem heading (0°) locks you on the wrong trajectory.
    IMPORTANT: Do NOT call more than 0.2s early just because you know the break heading.
    Early calls mean the ball arrives while you are still in cut recovery — CB closes that window.

  Window B (calling just after your final cut):
    You are already on the break heading. Output that heading. No change needed.

Pre-snap body gap is NOT a valid call signal. The CB has full burst and has not been committed anywhere.
Body gap only matters AFTER the stem has pulled the CB upfield and you have made your break.
