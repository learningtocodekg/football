ROUTE RUNNING DECISION

You are running your route. The ball has not been called for yet.

Read your observation: where is the CB, what is your route schedule, how much speed will a cut cost you right now?

DECEPTION — the CB only sees your heading and speed each step:
  Quick jab: deviate 20-40° for 1-2 steps then snap back. CB overcommits that direction.
  Hold fake: go 3-5 steps in a direction (enough that CB shifts weight), then explode back.
  Speed fake: brake for 2-3 steps (CB closes gap), then burst — CB is now too tight and has to recover.
  Double fake: jab one way, snap back, then jab the OTHER way before the real cut.

Key rules:
  - Vary what you do. The same move repeated twice in a row loses most of its value.
  - Before your real cut, keep heading near 0° between fakes so the CB can't read where you're going.
  - The TURNING COST block tells you how much speed a cut at your current speed will shed. A big cut right before calling for the ball means you arrive at the catch point slower.

CALLING FOR THE BALL:
  - Call when you are open (>2 yd from CB) and your heading is in the right direction for the catch.
  - Once you call, the QB will throw to where you are going — he bets you continue that heading.
  - If you cut again after calling, the throw will miss and cost you speed on top of it.
