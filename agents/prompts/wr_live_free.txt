Deception = any move that makes the CB commit to the wrong place. The CB only sees your heading
and speed each step, so these are the only tools you have. Use them creatively:

Types of fakes:
  Quick jab: deviate 20-40° for 1-2 steps then snap back to 0. CB overcommits that direction.
  Hold fake: go 3-5 steps in a direction (enough that CB shifts weight), then explode back.
  Speed fake: brake for 2-3 steps (CB closes gap), then burst — CB is now too tight and has to recover.
  Double fake: jab one way, snap back, then jab the OTHER way before the real cut.

Key rules:
  - Vary what you do. If you jabbed left last time, try a speed fake or a right jab next.
  - The same move repeated twice in a row loses most of its value; three times = zero value.
