Read your observation carefully. You have:
- Your note from last step (your scratchpad)
- Your current position, speed, heading, and BURST status
- steps_on_this_heading: how many consecutive steps your heading has been within ~20° of current (1 = just changed, 5 = held this direction for 5 steps)
- The CB's position, speed, heading, and BURST status
- The move log: both players' headings, speeds, and cut_recovery (rec) after each step

=== YOUR DECISION FRAMEWORK ===

STEP 1 — Read the burst status:
  - "CB BURST: RECOVERING — X steps left" → The CB cannot accelerate freely RIGHT NOW. This is separation time. Explode on your break.
  - "CB BURST: FULL burst available" → The CB is set and ready. Any route you run, they can match instantly. You need a fake first.
  - "YOUR BURST: RECOVERING" → You just cut. Don't expect instant speed. Let it clear, then explode.

STEP 2 — Read the move log rec column:
  - CB rec > 0 last step: your previous move cost the CB their burst. Keep the pressure on.
  - CB rec = 0 every step: CB has not been forced to cut. Your fakes are not landing. Change approach.
  - YOUR rec > 0: you just cut and are paying the price. Coast or brake, let it clear before another cut.

STEP 3 — Decide your action:
  - SELLING THE FAKE: Hold your fake direction. The CB's rec only triggers if THEY turn to match you.
    Check: did CB heading change this step? If yes, their rec will show next step. Hold one more step, then snap.
  - SNAPPING THE BREAK: Only snap when you see CB heading committed your fake direction AND CB rec > 0.
    Then burst hard on your real break direction — you have the window, use it.
  - SPEED FAKE: Brake for 2-3 steps. CB closes. Then burst — no recovery penalty since you were slow.
  - STUTTER STEP: Brake briefly before the cut to kill your own recovery penalty. Plant, then explode.

DECEPTION TAKES MULTIPLE STEPS:
- A one-step heading change is noise. The CB recalculates every step and ignores it.
- Only when the CB's BODY is moving the wrong direction (CB heading committed, CB rec > 0) have you actually fooled them.
- Check the move log: if CB rec shows 0 after your fake, the CB did not commit. Hold longer.
- FAKE DEPTH: If you want to fake left, your heading must reach approximately 270°. Right fake:
  approximately 90°. A 10-30° drift off your stem is not a fake — it does nothing to the CB's rec.
  Check the move log: if CB rec shows 0 after your "fake", the fake didn't work because it wasn't
  deep enough.

DECEPTION SERVES YOUR FINAL CUT — NOT GENERAL CONFUSION:
Every fake, jab, and direction change you make has ONE purpose: getting the CB's hips committed the
wrong way specifically for your FINAL BREAK DIRECTION. Design your deception around the cut you are
about to make. If your final break is 135°, fake toward ~315° first (opposite). If your final break
is 40°, fake toward ~220° first. Faking aimlessly or "generally confusing the CB" wastes steps.
Ask yourself each step: "Is what I'm doing right now going to make the CB's hips point the wrong
way when I snap to my FINAL break heading?" If the answer is no, you are wasting a step.

into a closing CB. The right moment to call is the step CB enters recovery, not after watching the
