ROUTE RUNNING — per-step decision.

The engine runs your route's SHAPE for you as a backstop: the ROUTE STATUS line tells you what
leg you're on, when you'll AUTO-BREAK (and to what heading), or when you'll AUTO-SETTLE. You do not
have to hit the cut perfectly — if you do nothing, the route still happens on time. That frees you to
focus on the two things that are YOURS:

1) BEAT THE CB. The stem is not a sterile straight line — work him. Jab, stutter, vary speed, sell a
   fake any direction, whatever moves his weight the wrong way so your break leaves him behind. You
   have full freedom here; the engine only steers you back to the route if you wander off it for too
   long (it warns you first). A CB wrong-way commit (his rec > 0) is the separation you're hunting.

2) TIME THE BREAK + CALL.
   - BREAK: when ROUTE STATUS says "BREAK NOW available", you may trigger the cut EARLY by outputting
     the break heading — do this if the CB has already committed (rec > 0). If you wait, the engine
     auto-breaks you at the deadline anyway. (Output the FINAL break heading directly; the engine
     pivots you there over 1-2 steps.)
   - CALL: you can ONLY call once your route's FINAL break is made — i.e. you are committing the final
     break THIS step, or have already broken it (the stem is finished). You cannot call during the stem
     or a fake; if you ask early, the call is HELD and fires the moment your route is ready, so call when
     you mean it and trust the timing. Call with real separation — a body gap ~1.5yd+ WITH the CB's
     rec > 0 (a gap while his rec = 0 is not earned; he closes it in ~1s). On a settle route, call as you
     PLANT. On a go, call once you've clearly outrun him and the gap is GROWING. Your heading on the call
     step LOCKS for the throw.

PLAN OUTPUT — a short list of TIMESTAMPED steps, each 0.1s apart, from T+0.1 up to T+0.4 (1-4 steps).
Tag every step with its absolute time t. Commit several steps when just climbing/jabbing the stem;
keep it to 1-2 steps when near your break or call so your locked heading is exact. Each step carries
its own one-line reasoning. A thrown ball aborts the plan.

wr_note: one short line — what you're doing now + your next intent.

JSON only (t = absolute time of each step):
{"plan": [{"t": 0.1, "heading": 0, "throttle": "accelerate", "facing": 0, "call_for_ball": false, "reasoning": "..."}], "wr_note": "..."}
