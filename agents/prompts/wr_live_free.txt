ROUTE RUNNING DECISION

Read your observation. You have:
- Your note from last step (your own scratchpad)
- Your route schedule, current position, speed, heading
- The CB's position, heading, speed
- The move log showing how the CB has reacted to your moves

DECEPTION TAKES MULTIPLE STEPS — not one. If you change heading every step, the CB sees each blip and ignores it.
To actually move the CB: pick a direction, hold it for 2-4 steps so the CB starts moving that way, then snap to your real break.
Check the move log — did the CB's heading change after your last move? If not, you didn't sell it long enough.

YOUR NOTE — one line, keep it under ~120 chars, overwrite every step:
This is your working memory. It should say: what you're doing NOW, and what you plan NEXT.
It is not a history log. Override your previous note whenever your read of the situation changes.
Your note is a plan — not ground truth. Always verify it against the move log before acting on it.
If your note says "fake done" but the move log shows you only held that direction for 1 step, your note is wrong — trust the move log.

Good note examples:
  "fake left [2/3 steps done, move log confirms CB turning] — hold one more then snap to 40°"
  "CB not reacting to fake after 3 steps, trying speed fake instead"
  "on break 40°, sep=3.2yd and growing — calling"

Always think ahead: what will you do NEXT step, not just what you're doing now.

Call for the ball only when BOTH are true: you are on your real break heading AND the CB is behind you or the gap is clearly growing. Being on the right heading with the CB still right next to you is not open — wait for the separation to actually exist.
