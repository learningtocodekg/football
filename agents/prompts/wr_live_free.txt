ROUTE RUNNING DECISION

You are running your route. The ball has not been called for yet.

Read your observation: where is the CB, what is your route schedule, how much speed will a cut cost you right now?

DECEPTION — the CB only sees your heading and speed each step:
  Quick jab: deviate 20-40° for 1-2 steps then snap back. CB overcommits that direction.
  Hold fake: go 3-5 steps in a direction (enough that CB shifts weight), then explode back.
  Speed fake: brake for 2-3 steps (CB closes gap), then burst — CB is now too tight and has to recover.
  Double fake: jab one way, snap back, then jab the OTHER way before the real cut.

READING CB REACTIONS — fakes only work if the CB reacts:
  - After each jab, look at the MOVE LOG. Did CB change heading? If CB Δhdg ≈ 0°, the CB IGNORED your fake.
  - A fake that the CB ignores has zero value. Do NOT repeat it.
  - One-step micro-jabs (20–30°) almost never make a CB commit. The CB needs to see you hold a direction for 2–3 steps before it reacts.
  - If your last 3 jabs all had CB Δhdg ≈ 0°, you are wasting time — switch to a bigger move or a speed change.
  - "Biting" does not mean a full sprint. Even a CB turning (10–20° heading shift) is a win — it costs the CB speed to recover.

Key rules:
  - Vary what you do. The same move repeated twice in a row loses most of its value.
  - Before your real cut, keep heading near 0° between fakes so the CB can't read where you're going.
  - The TURNING COST block tells you how much speed a cut at your current speed will shed. A big cut right before calling for the ball means you arrive at the catch point slower.

CALLING FOR THE BALL — ANTICIPATE, DON'T REACT:
  - Do NOT call based on current separation alone. The CB is always moving — it will close whatever gap you have right now.
  - Think ahead: where will YOU be in 1–2 seconds based on your route? Where will the CB be based on its current heading and speed?
    If your route takes you away from the CB's trajectory, separation is GROWING — good time to call.
    If the CB is tracking your direction, separation will SHRINK — wait for the cut first.
  - Call AFTER you have executed your route cut (heading near cut_heading). Only then are you actually open.
  - Call_for_ball=true is only valid if your current heading is within 45° of your cut direction.
    If you call before making the cut, the system will reject it.
  - Once you call, the QB throws to where you are going — he bets you continue that heading.
