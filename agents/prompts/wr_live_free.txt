ROUTE RUNNING DECISION

=== STRATEGIC FRAMEWORK — think through this every step ===

1. WHERE ARE YOU? Compare your current position to your snap position. How far into the route?
   Where does the route description say you should be right now?

2. WHERE IS THE CB? Upfield, behind, left, right of you? What heading is the CB running?
   Is the CB's body already committed one direction?

3. WHAT DO YOU NEED TO DO NEXT? Check YOUR HEADING NOW in the observation.
   Are you in the stem phase or already in the final break?

4. HOW MUCH DECEPTION DO YOU NEED?
   - Short routes (~5 yards): the stem IS the deception. One window is enough.
   - Longer routes (~10-12 yards): maybe one additional fake. No more.
   - Once the CB's hips are committed the wrong way, STOP faking and execute the route.
   - Constant deception wastes steps and costs you burst when you need it most.

5. HOW MUCH SEPARATION IS ENOUGH?
   Body gap >= 1.5 yards = high-probability catch. You do NOT need 5 yards.
   The moment you can get 1.5 yards on your break, the route worked — call and run.

6. WHEN TO CALL FOR BALL:
   Signal when you expect to be at MAX SEPARATION in the next 0.1-0.3 seconds.
   - If you are ABOUT TO MAKE your final cut: call now if you're confident the cut will open space.
   - If you JUST MADE your final cut: call as soon as you see the gap opening (CB rec > 0).
   - Do not wait to watch the gap grow visually — by then it's already closing.
   - The catch happens on or after the final cut. Never signal during the stem.

=== HOW DECEPTION WORKS ===

BURST STATUS — read this first:
  CB BURST "RECOVERING — X steps left" -> CB cannot accelerate freely. This is your window.
  CB BURST "FULL burst available" -> CB is set and can match any move instantly. Earn a window first.
  YOUR BURST "RECOVERING" -> you just cut and are paying the cost. Do not snap again yet.

MOVE LOG — read the rec column:
  CB rec > 0 last step: your previous move cost the CB their burst. You have a window.
  CB rec = 0 every step: your fakes are not landing. Fake wasn't deep enough, or CB isn't reacting.
  YOUR rec > 0: you just cut. Let it clear before another cut.

FAKE DEPTH:
  A 10-30 degree drift is noise — the CB ignores it. A real fake must reach ~270deg (left) or
  ~90deg (right). Check the move log: if CB rec stayed 0 after your "fake," it wasn't deep enough.

FAKE PURPOSE:
  Every fake has ONE goal: commit the CB's hips the WRONG way for your FINAL break direction.
  If your final break is 45deg, fake toward ~225deg (opposite) first.
  Faking aimlessly wastes steps and leaves the CB free to react.

=== CALL RULES ===

Call when you expect enough separation (body gap >= 1.5 yd) in the next 0.1-0.3 seconds. This means either:
  - You are one step away from making your final cut and confident it will open space, OR
  - You just made your final cut and CB rec > 0 (gap is peaking now)

The catch happens on or after the final cut — never during the stem.
