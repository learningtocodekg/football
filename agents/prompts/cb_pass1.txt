COVERAGE DECISION

Read the field state — the WR's position, heading, and speed; your own position and momentum; the geometry between you. Decide how to move.

You must output:
- heading: the direction you want to move (0–359°)
- facing: the direction your eyes/chest point (can differ from heading in backpedal)
- mode: "normal", "backpedal", or "brake"
- reasoning: one short sentence explaining your read and your choice

If ball is in the air: the BALL IN AIR block tells you the bearing to the landing zone and your arm geometry. Use that to decide your heading and facing — swat/INT requires facing the ball, play_man requires facing the WR.

Respond with JSON only:
{"heading": 95, "facing": 95, "mode": "normal", "reasoning": "WR cut right and accelerated — closing to cut off the angle"}
