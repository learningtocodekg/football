COVERAGE DECISION

Read the field state carefully. You have:
- Your position, speed, heading, and BURST status (cut_recovery)
- The WR's position, speed, heading, and BURST status
- Separation, bearing to WR, and WR's projected position 0.5s ahead
- Move history with rec columns showing cut_recovery after each step

=== YOUR DECISION FRAMEWORK ===

STEP 1 — Read the burst status first:
  - "WR BURST: RECOVERING — X steps left" → WR just cut. They cannot explode. CLOSE HARD NOW. This is a gift.
  - "WR BURST: FULL burst available" → WR is set. Be careful — they can go anywhere at full speed.
  - "YOUR BURST: RECOVERING" → You just turned wrong. Do NOT turn again — you will dig deeper into recovery.
    Instead: continue in your current direction, let it clear, then adjust once.

STEP 2 — Read the move log rec columns:
  - Your rec > 0: you were faked. Do not compound it. Stay disciplined in your current heading.
  - WR rec > 0 this step: close aggressively — they are slow right now.
  - WR rec = 0 every step: WR has not committed a big cut. Mirror patiently. Wait for the real move.

STEP 3 — Decide your move:
  - MIRRORING: Small heading adjustments (10-20°) toward the WR's projected position. Cheap, no recovery.
  - CLOSING: Large heading change toward WR when their rec > 0. Costly for you too, but worthwhile when WR is slow.
  - HOLDING: Stay in current heading when WR is running straight and you are well-positioned. Do not chase jabs.

PATIENCE RULE:
- If the WR changes heading once, do NOT match it with a large cut immediately. Wait one step.
- If they hold it for a second step, then it is real. Match it with a smooth angle, not a panic cut.
- One-step jabs are almost always fakes. Responding with a large cut = giving the WR what they want.

WHEN BALL IS IN AIR:
- The BALL IN AIR block tells you the bearing to the landing zone and your arm geometry.
- Use that to decide your heading and facing.
- Swat/INT requires facing the ball (within 60°), play_man requires facing the WR.
- Your cut_recovery still matters: if you are in recovery when the ball arrives, you cannot position well.

RESPONDING WITH JSON:
Output heading, facing, mode, and a short reasoning line.

Respond with JSON only:
{"heading": 95, "facing": 95, "mode": "normal", "reasoning": "WR cut right and accelerated — closing to cut off the angle"}
